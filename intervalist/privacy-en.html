Intervalist Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

H Labs Works ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application "Intervalist" ("the App"). This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. About the App

Intervalist lets you build menus from time blocks ("segments") and run them with a voice-guided timer. All menus, execution history, and settings you create are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. The App works fully offline, except for browsing and downloading from the public menu catalog (see 2.2 below).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information stored only on your device

All of the following is stored only in an on-device database (SQLite) and is never transmitted externally.

InformationPurposeStorage
Menus, segments, templatesCreating and running timer menusOn device only
Execution history and usage countsShowing past runsOn device only
App settings (voice, vibration, theme, language, etc.)App configurationOn device only
Purchase state (whether ads are removed)Applying the one-time purchaseOn device only

About backups: The export/import feature in Settings lets you save or restore the above data to a local file or via your device's share sheet, entirely at your initiative. We never receive this data.

2.2 Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, each of which may collect information independently.

Google AdMob (advertising)

  • To display ads, Google may collect the advertising ID (iOS: IDFA / Android: AAID), device information, approximate location, and app usage data.
  • On iOS, the advertising ID is used for tracking only if you grant consent via App Tracking Transparency (ATT). If you decline, non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Ads appear only on the menu list / editor screens and after a menu completes. No ads are shown during timer execution.
  • Details: Google Privacy Policy

GitHub Pages (public menu catalog delivery)

  • The App's "public menu" feature fetches static files (the menu catalog) that we publish on GitHub Pages (github.io, operated by GitHub, Inc.).
  • Fetches happen only when you open the catalog screen. The App never sends your account information, on-device data, or any other personal information — only plain HTTPS GET requests, the same as viewing a web page.
  • As with any web access, connection information such as your IP address may be logged by GitHub's servers. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

App Store / Google Play (in-app purchases)

  • The one-time purchase (remove ads, unlimited saved menus) is processed by the Apple / Google store. The App does not collect or transmit payment information or receipt values.
  • Purchase management and refunds follow each store's policies.

3. Location, Camera, and Microphone

The App does not request location, camera, or microphone permissions.

4. Crash and Usage Analytics

The App does not use any crash-reporting or usage-analytics service (such as Firebase), and does not collect data for those purposes.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases.

  • For advertising, the information described in section 2.2 is automatically sent to Google AdMob.
  • When required by law.
  • With your consent.

6. Opting Out of Personalized Ads

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • You can also remove ads entirely via the one-time purchase.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data the App stores (menus, history, settings, purchase state) is kept on your device.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all local data.
  • Deletion of data collected by Google AdMob follows Google's privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
