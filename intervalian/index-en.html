Intervalian

Build and run interval-style menus that bring rhythm and focus to your daily activities — training, study, or work — by breaking time into segments.

App Store (coming soon) Google Play (coming soon)

What it does

  • Menu & segment editor — combine time blocks ("segments") into a menu and save it
  • Voice-guided timer — Bluetooth headset support, countdown before each segment ends, vibration, and notifications keep you on rhythm
  • Routines — chain multiple menus together and run them in sequence (viewing/running is free; creating/editing requires Premium)
  • Execution history — review your past runs
  • 20 languages — Japanese, English, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Korean, Arabic (RTL supported), and more

Pricing

The App is free to use with ads, and all core features are available for free. A one-time Premium purchase unlocks:

  • No ads
  • Unlimited saved menus
  • Creating and editing Routines

The App works fully offline — all menus, history, and settings you create are stored on your device.
